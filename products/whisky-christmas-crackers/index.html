The Six Single Malts

Take a tour through the Scottish regions with six premium single malts

Isle of Islay

Laphroaig Lore

Strong phenols, rich oak

One of the most renowned Scotch whiskies around. This release has been created to honour the craftspeople who have shaped Laphroaig for centuries. Matured in five select cask varieties including ex-bourbon barrels, Oloroso sherry butts and quarter casks.

Isle of Islay

Kilchoman Rockside 11

Honeyed malt, warm smoke & tropical fruits

An 11-year-old single-farm single malt Scotch whisky featuring a delicate balance of bright citrus, tropical fruits, and light maritime smoke. Made using barley grown at the distillery!

Highlands

Aberfeldy 21 Pauillac Exceptional Cask Series

Red berries, wine and oak spice

Rich, fruity and complex. A premium-aged release from the Aberfeldy distillery. Honeyed sweetness balanced with oak tannins. Sweet, spicy with a long warming finish.

Isle of Mull

Ledaig Castaway

Caramalised banana, smoke, agave.

Ledaig is the peated expression from the Tobermory Distillery and this experimental release has been matured in rum and tequila casks!

Highlands

Glenturret Eclosion

Fruity and vibrant

This vibrant, deep yet playful limited edition from the Glenturret Distillery reflects the new era of the distillery under Lalique and Hansjörg Wyss. An unpeated malt matured predominantly in European Oak PX Sherry Casks with periods in American Oak.

Isle of Mull

Tobermory 21 Manzanilla

Dried fruits, orange and citrus. Christmas spices.

A premium release from the Tobermory Distillery on the Isle of Mull. This unpeated, elegant coastal expression is balanced with rich fruit and extended cask maturation.
