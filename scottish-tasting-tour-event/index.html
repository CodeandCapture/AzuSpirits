Exclusive blind tasting event

Scotch Whisky Tasting Evening

Join us at The Cridford Inn in Devon on 28th November to taste six premium single malt whiskies we hand selected at some of Scotland's finest distilleries.

Call 01626 853694

Including distillery exclusives and exceptional aged single malts, each whisky was personally selected by Azu Spirits founder Ellie during our Scottish Tasting Tour in August.

Join us for a unique blind tasting journey through Scotland, with stories from the distilleries and six remarkable single malts chosen for their quality, character and diversity.

What we'll taste

Presented as our signature blind tasting experience, the selected single malts include Tobermory 21 Manzanilla, Ledaig Castaway, Glenturret Eclosion, Aberfeldy 21 Exceptional Cask Pauillac Finish, Kilchoman 20 year and Laphroaig Lore.

We will provide Glencairn nosing glasses, tasting cards and the wax-sealed envelope to reveal which is which. £75 p/p includes six 25ml drams. Non-whisky-drinking companions are very welcome.
