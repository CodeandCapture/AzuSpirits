About Azu Spirits

We find exceptional things and create memorable ways to discover them.

Azu Spirits is an independent British spirits company built around curiosity, good taste and the pleasure of discovery. We curate exceptional Scotch whisky and British spirits, then create experiences that invite you to taste, compare, question what you think you know—and discover something worth knowing.

Our Story

Azu began with a fascination for why things taste the way they do.

Founder Ellie Asthana Gibson discovered the extraordinary diversity of single malt whisky while living in Glasgow in 2016. Working in whisky bars and at festivals led to writing about the science, production and stories behind Scotch; later, time behind the bar at Melbourne's 1806 deepened that fascination with spirits, flavour and hospitality.

Along the way, one particular ritual stood out. Within the drinks industry, tasting blind is commonplace: remove the label and suddenly reputation, price, age and expectation disappear. You're left with what's actually in the glass.

Why wasn't that experience more readily available to everyone?

That question became Azu Spirits.

We began by hosting tastings, before creating sharing boxes during 2020 and eventually developing our blind tasting gift collections. Today, every Azu collection is designed to bring some of that original experience into your home: carefully chosen spirits, stories worth discovering, a little anticipation—and the reveal waiting until the end.

Why Azu?

TASTE FIRST. LABEL SECOND.

Our bottles are numbered rather than identified, so the name on the label can't make the decision for you. Taste, compare and choose your favourites before opening the reveal. You may confirm everything you thought you knew—or find yourself completely wrong. That's part of the fun.

CHOSEN FOR A REASON

We don't simply assemble six miniatures. Every collection is curated as a tasting: exceptional spirits selected for their quality, character and the conversation they create together. From limited releases and distillery exclusives to bottles worth discovering from lesser-known producers, if it's in an Azu box, there's a reason we want you to try it.

THE STORY BEHIND THE GLASS

Taste is only the beginning. Distilleries, people, places, ingredients, casks and production all leave their mark. Our guides reveal the stories behind each spirit and explore why it tastes the way it does—without turning a good drink into a lecture.
